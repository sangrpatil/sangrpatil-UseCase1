📊 Approach & Query

Query Code


-- Query 4: Product distribution by category
SELECT 
    category,
    COUNT(DISTINCT fk_product_name) AS product_count,
    SUM(total_quantity_sold) AS total_quantity,
    SUM(total_revenue) AS total_revenue,
    ROUND(100.0 * SUM(total_revenue) / (SELECT SUM(total_revenue) FROM best_selling_products), 2) AS revenue_percentage
FROM best_selling_products
GROUP BY category
ORDER BY SUM(total_revenue) DESC;

Query Description

Executed SQL aggregation queries grouping transactions by product name and calculating: (1) Total quantity sold (SUM of total_quantity_sold), (2) Total revenue (SUM of total_revenue), (3) Market share percentage (revenue as % of total), (4) Average price per unit (revenue/quantity), (5) Revenue ranking across all products. Results were sorted by revenue and quantity to identify top performers and cross-validate best-seller status. Analysis included comparative rankings and competitive benchmarking against top 10 products by both revenue and quantity metrics.
